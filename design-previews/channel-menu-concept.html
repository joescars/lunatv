Design suggestion

Recommend 01, the Focused panel. It keeps the show and channel visible, groups Favorite / Record / Details as one predictable vertical list, and gives the Roku focus state enough room to read from the couch. Make Back the clear “resume watching” exit; keep channel surfing on Left / Right. Avoid putting seek/progress controls on this screen—live TV is a channel, not an on-demand video.

Concept only: the program, artwork, and action feedback are illustrative. No Roku code or playback behavior has been changed. Existing mappings today: OK shows/hides info, Down records, Star/Play toggles favorite, and Back exits to the guide.
